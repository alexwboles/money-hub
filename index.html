      No. 05

      How the data actually flows

      Two small bridges, both opt-in, both honest about their limits.

      Bridge A

      Spending → attack payment

      BudgetLens quantifies the savings its own nudges already name — subscriptions, dining, fees — and writes the monthly total to budgetlens.cashflow.v1. DebtPayoff reads it and offers it as your attack payment — "here's where your $150/mo could come from" — unless you've already set the slider yourself. Nothing is copied until you click.

      budgetlens.cashflow.v1 → debtpayoff.extra.v1

      Bridge B

      The household ledger

      This hub reads BudgetLens's monthly cash-flow and DebtPayoff's debt list — budgetlens.cashflow.v1 and debtpayoff.debts.v1 — and renders one ledger above. Same-origin localStorage only; host all four on one site (GitHub Pages project pages share an origin) or serve them from one local server.

      budgetlens.cashflow.v1 + debtpayoff.debts.v1 → this page

      The fine print

      These are independent products, not one platform. The bridges are a same-origin localStorage convention — if you run BudgetLens on a different domain than the hub, they can't see each other, and everything still works standalone. No forced connections, no cloud sync, no analytics. Full contract in the repo README.
